Starting point

Libelle is a human-powered aircraft built by the association Odonata e.V., where I led the propeller group. The team’s goal is to break the current distance world record in this class. The pilot can only sustain a few hundred watts, so every loss in the drivetrain is taken straight off the achievable distance. That makes the propeller a central part of the whole system.

Approach

Development is based on a parametric geometry model that allows the design parameters to be varied systematically. Three tools work together:

  • JavaProp for fast performance estimates and the initial blade design,
  • CAESES for parameterization and geometry optimization,
  • STAR-CCM+ for high-resolution CFD simulations based on the URANS equations.

Design and optimization ran iteratively: the fast methods narrow things down, CFD confirms and corrects.

Manufacturing

The blades are made as carbon half-shells in machined female molds for the pressure and suction side. Cutting, layup and laminating are done by hand.

Design targets

  • high aerodynamic efficiency across the relevant operating range,
  • robust performance throughout the flight envelope,
  • a structure at minimum weight: 800 g at 3.5 m diameter.
