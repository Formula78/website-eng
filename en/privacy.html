Hosting and server log files

This website is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation).

When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data that your browser transmits automatically, in particular:

  • IP address,
  • date and time of access,
  • page or file requested,
  • referrer URL,
  • browser type, operating system and device type.

This is required to deliver the website and keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I have no access to these log files and do not combine them with other data.

Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: GitHub privacy statement.

Encryption

The website is only delivered over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

Visitor statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

To understand which pages are used and how visitors reach this website, I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Your browser loads a script from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com) and transmits information about the page view, in particular:

  • page requested and time,
  • referrer URL,
  • browser type, operating system, device type and country of origin,
  • page loading times.

For technical reasons your IP address is also transmitted to Cloudflare. According to Cloudflare, the service uses no cookies or local storage, and individual visitors are not recognized by IP address, user agent or other data. I only see aggregated figures.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in designing my website according to demand (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object to this processing (Art. 21 GDPR). Technically, you can prevent it by blocking the script with a content blocker or in your browser settings.

Data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

Contact form (Web3Forms)

When you send the contact form, your details (email address and message, and optionally name, subject, company or project and timeframe) are forwarded to my mailbox by the service Web3Forms. The provider is Web3Creative, based in India. It processes the data on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

The provider uses Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner to run the service. To prevent spam, your IP address and email address may be sent to CleanTalk and Akismet (Automattic). According to the provider, form submissions are stored for no longer than three years and then deleted automatically, unless they are deleted earlier.

Data is transferred to countries outside the European Union, in particular India. There is no adequacy decision of the European Commission for India; according to the provider, the transfer relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). More information: Web3Forms privacy policy.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, otherwise my legitimate interest in answering inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Using the form is voluntary. You can email me directly instead, in which case Web3Forms is not used.

Contact by email

If you email me, I process the information you provide (e.g. name, email address, message content) to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, otherwise my legitimate interest in answering inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

I delete inquiries once they are dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. If a contract results, business correspondence is subject to German commercial and tax retention periods of up to ten years (§ 257 HGB, § 147 AO).

No cookies, no advertising

This website sets no cookies and uses no advertising services. Fonts, images, videos and its own scripts are loaded from this site’s own web space. The only third-party script is the one for the visitor statistics described above. No social media plugins, embedded videos or maps are used.

External links

The website contains plain links, including to LinkedIn and hpa-odonata.de. Only when you click such a link do you leave this website; from then on the privacy policy of that provider applies. No data is sent to these providers beforehand.

Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

Right to object

Where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to it at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your residence, place of work or the alleged infringement.

A short email to hohensee@coastl.de is enough for any request.
